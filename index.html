Everything, Styles, Patterns and Practices are the coarse filter — what a thing is. The trait filters are the columns of the trait matrix — what it is made of. Either narrows the atlas, the matrix and the grid, and the trait matrix carries its own Clear all filters whenever something is switched on.

Style specimens are drawn with the real technique each style is made of — no screenshots, no images, so what you copy is what renders. The real-world examples section is different by design: those are photographs of live sites, captured from the page itself by this repo's tooling, with the link out as the source of truth. The catalog chrome keeps to one neutral type ramp and a hairline-only surface treatment; the entries inside it deliberately break those rules, because breaking them is the entry.

Not everything here is a style. Thirty entries are looks, seven are screen or interaction patterns that could be built in any register, and six are practices that say how to work and mostly have no look of their own — the kind is stated on every card and in every detail, and the chip row filters by it. Within a kind nothing is bucketed: a style is placed on two axes — restraint against loudness, flat against dimension — and mapped against the recurring techniques in the trait matrix, so the traits two entries share are the thing you can see rather than the thing a bucket hides. On the atlas the names are the marks: nothing is drawn but the names, and where one had to move a hairline runs back to the spot it belongs to.

Style names describe visual conventions in broad use; origins are noted with each entry. Sources are listed per entry where a specific public write-up informed the description. Real sites are named and linked to their owners' pages, and are shown only as examples of the style.
